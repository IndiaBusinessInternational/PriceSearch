# PriceSearch v1.3

v1.3 (27 Oct 2026): edge to edge on every screen size - no centred 600px column on laptops and no 20px gutter on phones; full-width top bar and cards with 10px side padding; on wide screens the form is on the left and the market estimate on the right.

v1.2 (27 Oct 2026): WhatsApp link preview uses a 1200x630 JPEG banner (og-banner.jpg, image/jpeg); new "Share this app" button in the header.

Inputs given will be (1) Product Name (2) Product Image (3) Quantity. It should search online. Assume that Delivery is Free for all products and will be borne by the Seller. It should give outputs (1) Minimum Price (2) Maximum Price - in online marketplace. Useful for our eCommerce business to fix price of our products in those price ranges.
